DEVIATION REPORT

Site: Bangalore API Manufacturing Plant
Date of Occurrence: 27 September 2026

Batch Number: API-24091
Product: API-XYZ-500
Source: Manufacturing Process

Title: Temperature exceeded approved operating range

During the granulation stage, the product temperature exceeded
the approved operating range.

Approved temperature range: 70°C – 75°C.
Recorded temperature: 82°C.

The temperature remained at 82°C for approximately 18 minutes
before the operator identified the deviation and adjusted the
process parameter.

No equipment failure was observed.

The batch has been placed on hold pending investigation.
